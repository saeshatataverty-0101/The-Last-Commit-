            > Who can enter the game?+
            
Any student or self-taught dev, 16+. Beginners welcome — every class has a tutorial zone and mentors on comms.

          

          

            > What does it cost?+
            
Zero credits. Entry is free. Food, energy drinks, Wi-Fi, and nap pods are on the house.

          

          

            > Can I play solo?+
            
Squads are 2–4 operators. Solo? Hit the matchmaking lobby on Day 0 and we'll party you up.

          

          

            > Can I start coding before the event?+
            
No pre-built code. Every repo is created at git init and audited. Ideas & designs are fine.

          

          

            > What should I bring?+
            
Laptop, charger, ID, and the will to survive 48 hours. Sleeping bag optional but strongly recommended.
