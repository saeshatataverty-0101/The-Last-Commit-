          06 //
          
    FAQ
    
        
    
        
    
          
    
            > Who can participate?+
            
    Any student or self-taught developer aged 16+. Beginners are welcome, and every track has mentors available throughout the event.
    
          
    
          
    
            > What does it cost?+
            
    Nothing. Registration is free, and food, drinks, Wi-Fi and rest areas are provided.
    
          
    
          
    
            > Can I take part on my own?+
            
    Teams are 2–4 people. Coming alone? Register as solo and we'll help you find a team on Day 0.
    
          
    
          
    
            > Can I start coding before the event?+
            
    No. All code must be written during the event, starting from git init. Ideas and designs prepared in advance are fine.
    
          
    
          
    
            > What should I bring?+
            
    Laptop, charger, college or government ID, and anything you need to stay comfortable for 48 hours.
